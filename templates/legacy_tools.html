During the 2024 Q1 migration, legacy invoice records from the pre-2024 era were retained in a separate archive viewer while new invoices were assigned opaque public identifiers in the current system.

Legacy Reference Convention

Historical records were addressed by legacy reference numbers derived from their invoice number. The convention uses a stable, non-sequential identifier scheme so that reorganisation of invoice ids does not break historical lookups.

Description: Server maintenance performed on January 2024.

This record is from Bob's account and is not the target of the ongoing migration review. It is included here as a decoy example of the legacy reference format.

Internal Migration Memo (excerpt)

To: Nexus Vyoma Billing Team
From: Migration Ops
Date: 2024-03-01
Subject: Legacy viewer retention

The pre-2024 legacy invoice viewer remains available for historical reference during the Q1 migration period. Records addressed by legacy reference numbers will continue to be served through the /legacy/invoice/ endpoint. Account holders alice and bob both have historical records in the archive. The migration team is currently reconciling which legacy references correspond to which invoice numbers.

Note: Not all legacy references from the old system have been verified against the new invoice numbering. Some records may have been archived without a direct mapping. Treat all legacy references as potentially pointing to records outside the current user-owned set.

February Reconciliation Entry

The outstanding Bob account review concerns the February 15, 2024 invoice for $1,337.00. Its invoice number follows the B-series convention used for Bob's 2024 invoices. The reconciliation worksheet marks it as the second B-series invoice.

Reconciliation Notes

During the migration review, the following observations were made:
- Alice's three invoices (INV-2024-001 through INV-2024-003) have legacy references that map consistently to the new system.
- Bob's invoices have legacy references following the same convention.
- The legacy viewer addresses records by the original reference number, without ownership scoping. This is intentional and documented.
- Access to another user's record through the legacy viewer requires explicit authorization, which has not been implemented in the legacy code path.
- Participants investigating this workspace should focus on correlating information from the dashboard, notifications, and archive materials to identify the target record.
